From the transcript of a conversation, identify possible conversational missteps made **only by the participant named `{name}`**. For each issue, assign a **gravity score from 0 to 5**, where:

- **0** = no issue
- **5** = severe negative impact on the conversation or other participants

Provide a short observation including quotes as context, and if relevant, a culturally sensitive piece of advice (such as the one a socio-linguist might offer) to improve future interactions.

Everything must be phrased in a nice, neutral, non-judgemental way to avoid offending.

**Important guidelines:**
- Double-check that your observations correspond exactly to what appears in the transcript.
- Double-check the sequence of events to avoid confusions.
- Do **not** make assumptions about the participant’s gender.
- Only take into account mistakes that either make others feel bad or diminish the quality of the conversation.
- Always contextualize observations using direct quotes.
- Offer advice in a culturally considerate and universally empathetic manner.
- Be aware of possible instances of jokes or sarcasm used to make the conversation lighter or more fun.
- Double-check the results before returning your results to avoid hallucinations.
- For every metric evaluated below, more than one issue can be stated, that's why the returned JSON has an array for every metric.
- Verify the validity of the JSON return.

### Metrics to evaluate (ordered by their potential impact on conversational quality):

- **personal attacks**: Direct, hurtful comments targeting another participant personally.
- **contempt**: Remarks expressing disdain, mockery, or dismissiveness toward others.
- **meanness**: Remarks that are unnecessarily unkind or cause discomfort.
- **lack of empathy**: Comments showing disregard for others' feelings, struggles, or perspectives.
- **dismissal**: Ignoring, minimizing, or invalidating another participant’s contribution.
- **rudeness**: Disrespectful or inconsiderate remarks that breach basic conversational politeness.
- **interruptions**: Cutting someone off before they finish speaking, preventing idea completion.
- **criticism**: Negative remarks about others' ideas or behaviors. Differentiate between hurtful and constructive criticism.
- **unwilling to compromise**: Stubborn refusal to engage with alternative viewpoints or ideas.
- **not paying attention**: Comments that show a lack of engagement or connection with what others just said.
- **unconnected advice**: Giving unsolicited or irrelevant advice without considering the conversation’s flow or others' needs.
- **absolutism**: Expressing rigid, black-and-white thinking without nuance or openness.
- **assuming clarity**: Assuming others understood your point without checking for comprehension.
- **not good questions**: Asking questions that don’t contribute meaningfully to the discussion.
- **generic responses**: Offering vague, clichéd, or thoughtless replies lacking substance or engagement.
- **talking too much**: Domineering the conversation with overly lengthy or verbose interventions.
- **universal statements**: Making broad, generalized claims not grounded in the conversation’s context.
- **obvious statements**: Saying things self-evident without adding helpful context or framing.
